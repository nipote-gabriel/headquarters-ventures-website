Approach

The creator is the company.

Why we exist

Creators are the most under-capitalized founders on earth.

A creator with two million followers has a product, a distribution channel and a brand. They usually don’t have a CFO, a lawyer, or anyone helping them scale at the pace they should be.

Headquarters Ventures backs creators and entertainment properties like startups. We write early checks, then show up as the management team: brand deals, production, hiring, distribution, and strategy. When the venture grows, we grow with it.

How we back

From pre-viral to Series Clout.

01

Pre-Viral

A format, a voice and under 250K followers. We fund gear, editing and time, and help find the bit that works.

$500–$2.5k
02

Seed

Proven audience, no infrastructure. We step in as management: brand partnerships, contracts, a release schedule.

$2.5k–$10k
03

Series Clout

Millions of fans and a brand. We build the business around it: products, live tours, licensing, IP for TV and film.

Whatever’s left

What we look for

Three things. Sometimes two.

01

A point of view

We back people with a take, not a trend. If your content could be made by anyone, it will be.

02

A format

Something repeatable that can become a series, a show, a live event or a product line.

03

Stamina

Consistency beats virality. We want people who’ll still be posting in year five.
